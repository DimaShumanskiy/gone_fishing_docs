# Gone Fishing Support

Write to [gonefishing.support@gmail.com](mailto:gonefishing.support@gmail.com). We reply within a few days.

The easiest way is from the game: Help → Help tab → "Contact us". The email opens with your game version, device and player ID already filled in, which helps us sort things out faster.

## FAQ

**My progress is gone after switching phones.** Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used on the old phone: Settings → Account. Your progress will load from the server.

**A purchase didn't arrive.** Open Help → Help tab and tap "Restore purchases". If that doesn't help, send us the receipt from your app store.

**Notifications don't arrive.** Check that they are on in the game settings and in your phone settings for Gone Fishing.

**How do I delete my data?** Settings → Account → "Delete data on the server". More in the [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy.html' | relative_url }}).

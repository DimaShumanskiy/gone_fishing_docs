# Gone Fishing Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what data the Gone Fishing mobile game collects, why, who receives it, and how you can control it.

## Who we are

Gone Fishing is published by {{ site.owner }}, an individual developer in Minsk, Belarus ("we"). We are responsible for your personal data under this policy. Contact: [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## What we collect and why

You can play without giving us your name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We collect only the data below.

| Service | Data | Why | Their policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Our game server | Random device ID, game progress and settings, purchased items, IP address. If you sign in: account ID and email | Save your progress and restore it on a new device. The IP address tells us your country so we can pick an ad network that works there | |
| Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In (optional, not in the RuStore version) | Account ID and email. Apple may give us a private relay address | Sign you in. We show the email only so you can see which account you are using | [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| Google Firebase Analytics (with your consent) | Screens opened, buttons tapped, play time, where players stop playing, app instance ID, device model, OS version, approximate country | Find what is inconvenient in the game and fix it. No name or email | [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| Google Firebase Remote Config | App instance ID | Deliver game settings and test variants of the game, for example whether a banner is shown | same as above |
| Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (if you allow notifications) | Device token | Send notifications | same as above |
| Sentry | Error description, device model, OS and game version | Find and fix crashes | [sentry.io/privacy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) |
| RevenueCat (versions with purchases) | Random purchase ID, purchase history, store receipts | Confirm purchases and restore them | [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) |
| App Store, Google Play, RuStore | Payment details | Take payment. We never see your card details | the store's own policy |
| AppLovin MAX and its ad partners | Advertising ID (IDFA or GAID) if you allow it, device model, OS, IP address, ad views and taps | Show ads, count views, prevent ad fraud, and personalize ads if you agree | [legal.applovin.com/privacy](https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/) |
| Yandex Mobile Ads (RuStore version and players in Russia) | Same as AppLovin | Show ads | [Yandex Ads](https://yandex.com/legal/international_ads_privacy) |
| Yandex AppMetrica (RuStore version, instead of Firebase Analytics, with your consent) | Same as Firebase Analytics | Same as Firebase Analytics | [Yandex](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/) |

For more information about AppLovin's collection and use of your information visit: [https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/](https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/).

Each service above must protect your data at least as well as this policy requires. We do not sell your data for money. We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similar effects.

## Other disclosures

We also disclose data when the law requires it (for example, a court order), to protect players and ourselves from fraud and cheating, or to a company that takes over the game. In that last case this policy keeps applying to your data. We may publish statistics that cannot identify you.

## Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

- Contract: saving and syncing progress, sign-in, purchases. Without this data we cannot run your game.
- Consent: gameplay analytics, personalized ads, tracking on iPhone, notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect processing done before.
- Legitimate interests: crash reports, fraud prevention, and non-personalized ads, which pay for the free game.
- Legal obligation: keeping purchase records for tax and accounting.

## Ads and your choices

The game shows rewarded videos that you choose to watch. The Google Play version also shows a banner on the fishing screen.

- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for consent to personalized ads first. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized.
- On iPhone, ad networks get your advertising ID only if you allow tracking in the system prompt. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
- You can also ask us by email to stop personalized ads for your device.

## Notifications

Notifications stay off until you allow them. Turn them off in the game settings or in your phone settings.

## Children

Gone Fishing is for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you live in the EEA and are under the age of digital consent in your country, ask a parent before agreeing to analytics or personalized ads. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the data we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- restrict or stop some uses of it, including analytics and personalized ads, or object to uses based on our legitimate interests;
- withdraw your consent.

To delete your data on our server, open Settings → Account → "Delete data on the server" in the game. This deletes your account and the progress copy at once. For anything else, write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) from the game (Help → Contact us) so the message includes your player ID. We may ask you to confirm the data is yours. We reply within one month.

Deleting the game from your phone does not delete data on our server. Stores and ad networks hold their own data; use their settings or contact them.

In the EEA and UK you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live, work, or where you think the breach happened.

## US residents

In the last 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (device ID, account ID, email, advertising ID, IP address), commercial information (purchase history), internet activity (gameplay events, ad views and taps) and approximate location (country). We collect them from you and your device, for the purposes in the table above. We do not collect sensitive personal information.

We do not sell personal information for money. Letting ad networks use your advertising ID for personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under California and other state laws. To opt out, decline tracking on iPhone, delete your advertising ID on Android, or email us. Depending on your state, you also have the right to know, access, correct and delete your data. Using these rights will not change how the game treats you. An authorized agent can send a request for you with your written permission. We do not give your data to others for their own direct marketing. We do not knowingly sell or share data of users under 16.

## How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Progress copy and account | Until you delete them, or 24 months after you last open the game |
| Analytics | 14 months |
| Crash reports | 90 days |
| Purchase records | As long as tax law requires |
| Notification token | Until you turn notifications off or delete the game |

## Where data is processed

Our server and the services in the table may process your data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, for example from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on adequacy decisions (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or standard contractual clauses. Write to us for a copy.

The RuStore version has no sign-in. Progress stays on the phone, and our server gets only a copy linked to a random ID, without name or email.

## Security

The connection to our server is encrypted (HTTPS), and access to the server is restricted. No system is fully secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the game.

# Gone Fishing Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This page explains what data Gone Fishing collects, why, and how to opt out. Questions: [gonefishing.support@gmail.com](mailto:gonefishing.support@gmail.com).

## What we collect

**Game progress.** Your catch, level, tackle, coins and settings. They are stored on your device, and a copy goes to our server so you don't lose progress when you change phones. The copy is tied to a random device ID; it contains no name or phone number.

**Account, if you sign in with Apple or Google.** We receive the account ID and the email address Apple or Google shares with us. We use the email only to show which account you are signed in with. You can play without signing in; your progress is then tied to the device.

**Gameplay statistics.** Which screens open, which buttons are tapped, how long you play, where players quit. We collect this with Google Firebase Analytics to find what is inconvenient in the game and fix it. The data contains no name or email.

**Crash reports.** If the game crashes, Sentry receives the error description, device model, OS version and game version. The report contains no personal data.

**Purchases.** Payments are handled by the store you installed the game from (App Store, Google Play or another store); we never see your card details. RevenueCat tells the game a purchase went through and keeps your purchase history so purchases can be restored.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a token to deliver them. You can turn notifications off in the game settings or in your phone settings.

**Ads.** Ads in the game are selected by AppLovin MAX together with partner ad networks such as Google AdMob. They may use your device's advertising ID to choose ads. On iPhone this happens only after you allow it in the system prompt. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game first asks for consent to personalized ads; if you decline, ads are shown without personalization. Premium and Fishing Club players see no ads.

## Who we share data with

Only the services the game needs to work:

| Service | Purpose | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging) | statistics, notifications | [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| AppLovin MAX and partner networks | ads | [applovin.com/privacy](https://www.applovin.com/privacy/), partner list on request |
| Sentry | crash reports | [sentry.io/privacy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) |
| RevenueCat | purchases | [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) |
| Apple, Google, app store | sign-in and payments | their own policies |

We do not sell player data.

## How long we keep data

Progress and account data are kept until you delete your account. Statistics are kept for up to 14 months, crash reports for up to 90 days.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data. You can delete your server data in the game: Settings → Account → "Delete data on the server". This removes your account and the progress copy. If you need help, write to [gonefishing.support@gmail.com](mailto:gonefishing.support@gmail.com); we reply within 30 days.

## Children

The game is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you learn that a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
